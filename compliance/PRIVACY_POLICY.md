# Wisdom Eye of Obwiler · Privacy Policy

## 一、我们收集的信息 / 1. Information We Collect

奥贝之眼是一款 Android 相机应用，我们在使用过程中**不会**主动收集、上传或存储您的个人信息到我们的服务器。应用仅处理以下本地数据：

- **照片数据**：应用使用相机拍摄的照片，这些照片默认保存在您设备的本地存储中 (`/sdcard/Pictures/WEO/`)。
- **用户配置**：您手动输入的第三方 AI 服务 API Key 及服务商配置信息，保存在您设备的本地存储中 (`/sdcard/WEO/config.json`)。
- **权限使用**：应用需要以下系统权限以正常运行：
  - `CAMERA` — 用于拍摄照片
  - `INTERNET` — 用于将照片发送至您指定的第三方 AI 服务商

本应用**不包含**任何分析、埋点或遥测功能。我们不会追踪您的使用行为。

---

Wisdom Eye of Obwiler is an Android camera application. We **do not** actively collect, upload, or store your personal information on our servers. The app only processes the following local data:

- **Photo Data**: Photos taken using the camera are saved by default to your device's local storage (`/sdcard/Pictures/WEO/`).
- **User Configuration**: The third-party AI service API key and provider configuration you manually enter are saved to your device's local storage (`/sdcard/WEO/config.json`).
- **Permissions**: The app requires the following system permissions to function:
  - `CAMERA` — to take photos
  - `INTERNET` — to send photos to the third-party AI service provider you specify

This app does **not** include any analytics, tracking, or telemetry. We do not track your usage behavior.

---

## 二、我们如何使用信息 / 2. How We Use Information

您的数据仅在本地设备上使用，用于以下目的：

- **拍摄与存储照片**：将相机拍摄的图片保存至您的设备本地。
- **连接第三方 AI 服务**：根据您的配置，将照片发送至您指定的第三方 AI 服务商（如 OpenAI、Google 等）进行分析。此过程**完全由您在设备端发起**，照片直接发送至第三方服务商，不经过我们的服务器。

我们**不会**将您的照片或配置用于任何其他目的，也不会进行任何形式的数据挖掘、训练或分析。

---

Your data is used solely on your local device for the following purposes:

- **Taking and Storing Photos**: Save images captured by the camera to your device's local storage.
- **Connecting to Third-Party AI Services**: Based on your configuration, send photos to the third-party AI service provider you specify (e.g., OpenAI, Google) for analysis. This process is **entirely initiated on your device** — photos are sent directly to the third-party provider and do not pass through our servers.

We **do not** use your photos or configuration for any other purpose, nor do we perform any form of data mining, training, or analysis.

---

## 三、数据存储 / 3. Data Storage

- **照片**：拍摄的照片保存在您设备的 `/sdcard/Pictures/WEO/` 目录下。照片完全由您控制，您可以随时通过系统文件管理器或相册应用删除。
- **配置信息**：API Key 及服务商配置保存在 `/sdcard/WEO/config.json` 文件中。卸载应用时请注意手动删除该文件（如需要）。
- **我们无权访问**您的任何本地存储数据。所有数据仅存在于您的设备上。

---

- **Photos**: Taken photos are saved in the `/sdcard/Pictures/WEO/` directory on your device. Photos are fully under your control — you may delete them at any time via the system file manager or gallery app.
- **Configuration**: API keys and provider configuration are saved in `/sdcard/WEO/config.json`. Please manually delete this file when uninstalling the app if desired.
- **We have no access** to any of your locally stored data. All data resides exclusively on your device.

---

## 四、第三方服务 / 4. Third-Party Services

奥贝之眼支持用户自行配置第三方 AI 服务商。当您使用此功能时：

- 照片将根据您的指令发送至**您指定的**第三方 AI 服务商。
- 第三方服务商对您数据的处理受其各自的隐私政策约束，与奥贝之眼无关。
- 我们建议您在使用前仔细阅读相关第三方服务商的隐私政策。
- 您应在 Android 系统权限管理中确保仅授予应用必要的权限。

本应用不集成任何第三方 SDK（广告、统计、社交等），唯一的网络通信是您主动发起的对 AI 服务商的 API 调用。

---

Wisdom Eye of Obwiler supports user-configured third-party AI service providers. When you use this feature:

- Photos are sent, at your direction, to the third-party AI service provider **you specify**.
- The third-party provider's handling of your data is governed by their respective privacy policy and is independent of Wisdom Eye of Obwiler.
- We recommend reviewing the relevant third-party provider's privacy policy before use.
- You should ensure only necessary permissions are granted to the app via Android system permission management.

This app does not integrate any third-party SDKs (advertising, analytics, social, etc.). The only network communication is the API call to the AI service provider that you actively initiate.

- **知情权**：了解我们如何处理您的个人信息。
- **决定权**：限制或拒绝我们对您个人信息的处理。
- **查阅复制权**：查阅、复制您的个人信息。
- **删除权**：请求删除您的个人信息（您的数据存储于本地，您可以直接操作删除）。
- **投诉举报权**：向监管部门投诉。

由于所有数据处理均在本地完成且我们不会收集您的个人信息，您对自己的数据拥有完全的控制权。

---

Under the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China (PIPL), you have the following rights:

- **Right to Know**: Understand how we process your personal information.
- **Right to Decide**: Restrict or refuse our processing of your personal information.
- **Right to Access and Copy**: Access and copy your personal information.
- **Right to Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal information (your data is stored locally and you can delete it directly).
- **Right to Complain**: File complaints with regulatory authorities.

As all data processing occurs locally and we do not collect your personal information, you have full control over your data at all times.
